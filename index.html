Features

Everything at a glance.
Nothing in the cloud.

A focused desktop app built around one idea: your portfolio is your business, so it should live on your machine.

Runs locally

A native Windows desktop app. Your wallet data is stored on your own computer, not on someone else's server.

Live market prices

Prices, 24h change and 7-day charts refresh every minute — and fall back gracefully when you're offline.

Portfolio insights

Portfolio chart and allocation donut show where your value sits and how it moves over time.

Send, receive & exchange

Per-asset wallets with send and receive flows, QR codes, and a built-in exchange screen.

Privacy mode

Hide every balance with one click — handy when you're sharing your screen or working in public.

Installer or portable

Install it like any app, or grab the portable .exe and run it straight from a folder or USB stick.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does "runs locally" mean?

Nebula is a desktop app. Your wallet data and settings are stored on your own computer. The only thing it fetches from the internet is public market price data.

Why do I need an invite code?

Nebula is in early access, so we're keeping the group small while we polish things. If someone shared a code with you, you're in.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or login. Just install and open.

Which operating systems are supported?

Windows for now, as an installer or a portable .exe.

Windows says the app is unrecognized. Is that normal?

Early builds aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. Only download Nebula from this site.
